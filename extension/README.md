# CIVIX Pocket Device — Chrome Extension (Side Panel)

This extension puts the **CIVIX Pocket Device** directly inside Google Chrome's browser Side Panel. While playing Parlamentum or trading on partner sites, you have your pocket device, universal single sign-on, and live edicts docked right on your screen.

---

## How to Install (Load Unpacked)

1. Open **Google Chrome** and navigate to:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions
   ```
2. Enable **Developer mode** using the toggle switch in the top-right corner.
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left.
4. Select this directory:
   ```text
   g:\civix\extension
   ```
5. Pin the **CIVIX Pocket Device** icon to your Chrome toolbar.
6. Click the CIVIX toolbar icon — the Pocket Device will slide open directly in your Chrome Side Panel!

---

## Features
- **Always 1-Click Away:** Keep your stock positions, bank vaults, and job contracts docked next to any tab.
- **Dynamic Badge Count:** Toolbar icon displays active unread edict counts.
- **Embedded Sandbox:** Launch partner apps in expand mode directly alongside the game.
